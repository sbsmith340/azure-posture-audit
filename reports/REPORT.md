## Findings by check

### IAM-02 Legacy authentication is blocked (High)

**Why it matters:** Legacy protocols (IMAP, POP, SMTP AUTH, older Office clients) cannot do MFA, so they bypass it.

**Fix:** Enable a Conditional Access policy for All users with client apps 'Exchange ActiveSync' and 'Other clients' and grant control 'Block'.

- `tenant`: A legacy-auth block policy exists but is not enforced: 'CA002 Block legacy authentication' (enabledForReportingButNotEnforced).

### IAM-04 No Conditional Access policies left in report-only or disabled (Low)

**Why it matters:** Report-only and disabled policies look like protection on a dashboard but enforce nothing.

**Fix:** Review sign-in impact in report-only mode, then switch the policy to On, or delete it.

- `CA002 Block legacy authentication`: state is enabledForReportingButNotEnforced
- `CA004 Require compliant device for Exchange Online`: state is disabled

### IAM-05 Between two and four Global Administrators (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** Microsoft recommends fewer than five Global Administrators; more widens the blast radius of a compromise, and fewer than two risks lockout.

**Fix:** Move day-to-day admins to least-privileged roles and keep two break-glass Global Administrators.

- `tenant`: 5 permanent Global Administrators: aisha.khan@contosofield.example, break-glass-01@contosofield.example, maria.lopez@contosofield.example, old.admin@contosofield.example, tom.becker@contosofield.example.

### IAM-06 Privileged roles are PIM-eligible, not permanent (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** Standing admin rights are always available to an attacker; just-in-time activation limits the window.

**Fix:** Convert permanent privileged assignments to eligible assignments in Privileged Identity Management, keeping only break-glass accounts permanent.

- `maria.lopez@contosofield.example`: permanent Global Administrator
- `tom.becker@contosofield.example`: permanent Global Administrator
- `old.admin@contosofield.example`: permanent Global Administrator
- `aisha.khan@contosofield.example`: permanent Global Administrator
- `partner.auditor_fabrikam.example#EXT#@contosofield.example`: permanent Exchange Administrator

### IAM-08 No enabled accounts inactive for 90 days (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** Unused accounts are rarely monitored, so they are a common foothold.

**Fix:** Disable accounts with no sign-in for 90 days after confirming with the owner; review service accounts separately.

- `old.admin@contosofield.example`: last sign-in 2026-03-02 (213 days)
- `sam.reed@contosofield.example`: last sign-in 2026-05-10 (144 days)
- `svc-scanner@contosofield.example`: enabled and has never signed in

### IAM-09 No guest accounts hold privileged roles (High)

**Why it matters:** Guests are governed by another organization's identity controls and offboarding.

**Fix:** Remove the role, or give the person a member account governed by your own policies.

- `partner.auditor_fabrikam.example#EXT#@contosofield.example`: guest holds Exchange Administrator

### IAM-10 Users cannot consent to applications on their own (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** Illicit consent grants give an attacker's app persistent mailbox or file access that survives a password reset.

**Fix:** Set user consent to 'Do not allow' (or verified publishers with low-risk permissions only) and turn on the admin consent workflow.

- `tenant`: user consent policy: ManagePermissionGrantsForSelf.microsoft-user-default-legacy

### APP-02 App secrets are valid for no more than 365 days (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** Long-lived secrets are the ones that end up in scripts and repositories.

**Fix:** Issue secrets for 12 months or less, or replace them with certificates or managed identities.

- `Legacy Mail Relay`: secret lifetime 897 days

### APP-03 No apps hold high-risk Microsoft Graph application permissions (High)

**Why it matters:** Application permissions act without a signed-in user; Directory.ReadWrite.All or Mail.ReadWrite on a compromised app is tenant-wide.

**Fix:** Replace with least-privilege or resource-scoped permissions (for mail, an Exchange application access policy or RBAC for Applications), and review owners and credentials of apps that keep them.

- `HR Directory Connector`: granted Directory.ReadWrite.All
- `Legacy Mail Relay`: granted Mail.ReadWrite, Mail.Send

### NET-01 No SSH or RDP open to the internet (High)

**Why it matters:** Internet-exposed management ports are brute-forced within minutes of exposure.

**Fix:** Remove the rule and use Azure Bastion or just-in-time VM access; if a rule must stay, restrict the source.

- `nsg-jump`: rule 'AllowRdpAnywhere' allows RDP from *
- `nsg-build`: rule 'AllowMgmtPorts' allows SSH from 0.0.0.0/0

### STG-01 Storage accounts block anonymous blob access (High)

**Why it matters:** Anonymous access exposes any container someone later marks public.

**Fix:** Set allowBlobPublicAccess to false on the account.

- `stpublicforms01`: allowBlobPublicAccess is enabled

### DEF-01 Defender for Cloud plans are on for core workloads (Moderate)

**Why it matters:** The free tier gives posture recommendations only; threat detection needs the paid plan per resource type.

**Fix:** Enable the Standard tier for VirtualMachines, SqlServers, StorageAccounts, KeyVaults.

- `StorageAccounts`: plan is Free
